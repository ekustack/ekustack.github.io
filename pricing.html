Frequently asked questions

What happens after my free trial?

There is no time‑limited trial. The Free plan is permanent for single‑series charts. You only pay when you need Pro features.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade anytime. Your charts remain intact, and you only pay the difference.

Do you offer a student discount?

Yes, students can get 40% off Pro. Email us from your .edu address and we'll set it up.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards via Paystack. You can also pay in NGN if you're in Nigeria.

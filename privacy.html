Privacy Policy

Effective: August 19, 2026

ProvChart (operated by DevTemple) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service. Please read it carefully.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your email address, name, and password (hashed). This information is shared with D‑invites, which provides authentication for ProvChart.
  • Usage Data: We log which chart types you generate, how many series and points you use, and the frequency of your requests. This helps us improve the Service and understand usage patterns.
  • Payment Information: All payments are processed by Paystack. We do not store your credit card details or full payment data — only your subscription status, plan, and expiration date.
  • Cookies and Tracking: We use essential cookies to maintain your session and preferences. We do not use third‑party tracking cookies for advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • Authenticate you and manage your account.
  • Process your subscription payments.
  • Send you service updates and important notices (e.g., billing, security).
  • Analyse usage patterns to improve our features and performance.
  • Respond to your support requests and feedback.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We share your information only in the following cases:

  • With D‑invites: Since ProvChart uses D‑invites for authentication, your account information is shared with the D‑invites system (also operated by DevTemple).
  • Service Providers: We use Supabase for database hosting, Paystack for payment processing, and Cloudflare Workers for our backend API. These providers have access to the data necessary to perform their functions but are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, we may disclose your information to comply with a court order, subpoena, or other legal process.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers operated by Supabase, a compliant database provider. We use industry‑standard encryption (SSL/TLS) for data transmission and store passwords in hashed form. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Our backend API runs on Cloudflare Workers, which provides additional security and performance features.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal, tax, or regulatory purposes. Usage logs may be retained in anonymised form for analytical purposes.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You can correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: You can request that we delete your personal data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Restriction: You can ask us to restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Data Portability: You can request a copy of your data in a structured, machine‑readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at provchart@devtem.org. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and to remember your preferences (such as theme selection). These cookies are strictly necessary for the Service to function. We do not use analytical or marketing cookies that track your behaviour across other sites.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect the functionality of the Service.

8. Third‑Party Links

Our Service may contain links to third‑party websites (e.g., GitHub, Paystack). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

9. Children’s Privacy

ProvChart is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that we have collected data from a child without parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed on servers located outside your country. We use providers (Supabase, Cloudflare) that comply with international data protection standards. By using the Service, you consent to such transfers.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the effective date. Your continued use of the Service after the changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

Email: provchart@devtem.org
Website: devtem.org
GitHub: fscss-ttr
